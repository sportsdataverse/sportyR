# Articles

### All vignettes

- [Animating Tracking
  Data](https://sportyR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/animating-tracking-data.md):

  Turning tracking coordinates into an animated play, combining
  `sportyR` surfaces with `gganimate`.

- [Customizing
  Plots](https://sportyR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/customize-plot.md):

  Recolouring, resizing and re-orienting a `sportyR` surface, overriding
  the league defaults to match a team palette or a different coordinate
  convention.

- [Plotting Tracking
  Data](https://sportyR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/plotting-tracking-data.md):

  Overlaying player and ball tracking coordinates onto a `sportyR`
  surface, including how to align a provider’s coordinate system with
  the plot.

- [Getting
  Started](https://sportyR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/sportyR.md):

  Drawing regulation playing surfaces for a dozen sports from a single
  call -
  [`geom_baseball()`](https://sportyR.sportsdataverse.org/reference/geom_baseball.md),
  [`geom_hockey()`](https://sportyR.sportsdataverse.org/reference/geom_hockey.md),
  [`geom_basketball()`](https://sportyR.sportsdataverse.org/reference/geom_basketball.md)
  and the rest of the family.
